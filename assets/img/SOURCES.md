## Hero film and poster

Generated using Higgsfield Seedance 2.5, job `10b18adc-d183-413f-b7df-d0f6c3538eac`,
with `home-interior.jpg` as the authorized architectural reference. The poster is
a frame from that film; the site labels this as AI architectural inspiration.
The delivered 8-second loops use a closed forward/reverse path and have no audio.
